HON. MAVIS OHENE BOAKYE

National Director for Projects, Programs & Logistics

HON. MAVIS OHENE BOAKYE serves as the National Director for Projects, Programs & Logistics of Youth Parliament Ghana. She supports the planning, coordination and implementation of impactful youth projects, programs, national sittings, summits and logistical operations across the organization.

Working directly under the Office of the President of Youth Parliament Ghana, the Directorate supports effective program delivery, youth participation in governance, civic leadership and community development.

NATIONAL DIRECTORATE FOR PROJECTS, PROGRAMS & LOGISTICS

Directorate Mandate

The Directorate coordinates projects, programs, national parliamentary activities and logistical operations that support the work and development of Youth Parliament Ghana.

Projects, Programs & Development

Conceptualize, plan and execute national projects and youth empowerment programs aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and Ghana's youth development agenda.

National Sittings & Summits Coordination

Coordinate the planning and execution of parliamentary sittings, leadership training camps and national youth summits of Youth Parliament Ghana.

Logistics & Operations Management

Manage end-to-end logistics for official delegations, including accommodation, transportation, venue setup and delegate welfare.
