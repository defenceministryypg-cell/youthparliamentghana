About Us

Learn more about Youth Parliament Ghana, our purpose, values and commitment to developing young people through leadership, representation and civic participation.

WHO WE ARE

Building a Generation of Responsible Leaders

Youth Parliament Ghana provides a platform through which young people can engage with leadership, parliamentary processes, public policy and civic responsibilities.

The platform is designed to encourage young people to understand democratic institutions, develop leadership skills and participate meaningfully in discussions concerning their communities and the nation.

Promoting youth leadership and representation.
Encouraging civic education and participation.
Creating opportunities for constructive dialogue.
Supporting responsible and ethical leadership.
OUR PURPOSE

Mission & Vision

Our work is centred around youth participation, leadership development and civic responsibility.

Our Mission

To create meaningful opportunities for young people to develop leadership skills, understand parliamentary processes and contribute constructively to civic and national development.

Our Vision

To nurture a generation of informed, responsible and active young leaders who are prepared to contribute positively to their communities and Ghana's future.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our Core Values

The principles that guide our approach to youth leadership and civic engagement.

Integrity

Promoting honesty, accountability and responsible conduct.

Inclusion

Creating opportunities for young people to participate and be heard.

Innovation

Encouraging new ideas and constructive approaches to leadership.

Service

Encouraging young leaders to serve their communities with purpose.
