FOUNDER & PRESIDENT, YOUTH PARLIAMENT GHANA

HON. OSMAN ABDUL LATIF CHEBOO

Founder and President, Youth Parliament Ghana

Hon. Osman Abdul Latif Cheboo is the Founder and President of the Youth Parliament of Ghana, an independent and non-partisan youth leadership institution established to create a platform where young Ghanaians can learn, practice and participate meaningfully in democratic leadership.

His leadership is built around one simple belief: young people should not only be prepared for the future, they should be given the opportunity to contribute to the present.

Through the Youth Parliament of Ghana, Hon. Osman Abdul Latif Cheboo has brought together young people from constituencies, regions, schools and communities across the country to participate in parliamentary activities, leadership development, policy discussions and civic engagement.

Under his leadership, the Youth Parliament has developed a national structure involving 276 Youth Members of Parliament, alongside ministers, deputy ministers, regional ministers and other youth leadership positions.

The institution provides young people with practical exposure to parliamentary processes, public policy, leadership and national development.

As President, H.E. Osman is responsible for providing strategic leadership to the institution, coordinating the national structure, working with parliamentary and institutional stakeholders, and ensuring that the Youth Parliament remains focused on its mission of developing responsible, knowledgeable and service-oriented young leaders.

His vision is to build a generation of young leaders who can debate ideas without hatred, disagree without violence, serve without discrimination and place the national interest above personal interests.
